Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 26, 2026

1. Overview

Stache Caddie ("we," "our," or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile and desktop applications handle information when you use Stache Caddie alongside your golf simulator software.

In short: Stache Caddie does not collect, sell, or monetize your personal information.

2. Local Network Communication

Stache Caddie is engineered as a zero-configuration local companion application:

  • All communication between the mobile client and the host computer runs strictly over your Local Area Network (LAN) via private TCP and UDP protocols.
  • Your simulator inputs, aiming coordinates, shot replays, and mulligan commands never leave your private local Wi-Fi network.
  • We do not operate intermediate relay servers or store your gameplay data on the cloud.

3. In-App Purchases & Billing Information

Stache Caddie offers an optional one-time purchase ("Stache Caddie Pro") to unlock advanced simulator features:

  • Payment Processing: All financial transactions and payment details (such as credit card numbers or billing addresses) are handled directly by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. Stache Caddie never sees, processes, or stores your payment card credentials.
  • Receipt Validation: We utilize RevenueCat to securely validate store purchase receipts and maintain your active entitlement state. RevenueCat receives anonymous transaction identifiers generated by Apple/Google to confirm that your purchase is authentic.
  • Offline Enclave Caching: Once verified, your Pro status is securely cached in your device's hardware-backed local encrypted storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore) so the application functions seamlessly offline without contacting external servers.

4. Analytics and Tracking

Stache Caddie does not use third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or cross-app tracking mechanisms. We do not track your location or profile your activity across other apps or websites.

5. Children's Privacy

Our applications do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date." We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
